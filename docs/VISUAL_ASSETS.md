# Cinematic backgrounds

The shared cockpit system in `frontend/src/cockpit.css` adds cyan illumination, lime actions, violet accents, glass surfaces and perspective orbits across public, authentication, evaluation and operations pages. `NetworkHologram.tsx` renders a lightweight decorative SVG concept view; live topology continues to use controller-discovered records. The sign-in and registration forms share the same identity shell. Motion can be paused across routes and honors the operating system's reduced-motion preference.

These decorative backgrounds were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and converted to WebP in desktop and mobile sizes. The photos illustrate the project. Measurements, topology, and model results come from the SDN API.

| Asset | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| [Fiber](../frontend/public/assets/scene-fiber.webp) | Cinematic macro photograph of real fiber-optic strands sweeping from the lower-right foreground toward the distance. Lime, white and teal filaments over graphite, with a dark left third and no graphics or text. |
| [Rack](../frontend/public/assets/scene-rack.webp) | Low-angle photograph of black SDN switch racks, tidy cables, green indicators and a cool blue ceiling. Bright practical light, realistic reflections, no people or digital graphics. |
| [Silicon](../frontend/public/assets/scene-silicon.webp) | Macro photograph of a network ASIC and metallic traces on graphite, with a chip at the lower right and controlled lime and mint highlights. Dark negative space on the left, no lettering or UI. |

Use the pause icon in the page header or workspace toolbar to pause scroll-driven effects. The preference applies across routes. The operating system’s reduced-motion setting also pauses these effects.
